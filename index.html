Meet the Founder

A vision rooted in
Global fashion + Indian roots

Aishwarya Agarwal

Hi, I’m Aishwarya Agarwal. For over 15 years, I’ve built platforms that bring designers and dreamers together, guided by a deep love for Indian fashion and the creative minds shaping it.

With Design District, I’m not curating another show. I’m opening a new chapter. A space where fashion meets purpose, talent is celebrated, and every label tells a story that goes beyond trends.

For designers, it’s a stage before an engaged audience that values originality. For shoppers, it’s a chance to meet the creators and take home pieces that feel personal. Design District isn’t just a destination. It’s discovery, connection, and conversation. More than an event, it’s a vision to create experiences that linger long after the lights fade.

I invite you to be part of this journey — because when we come together, we don't just elevate design. We reshape culture.

Aishwarya
